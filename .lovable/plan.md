

# Haus of Posh — Event Decorator Website

A luxurious, modern website for an event decoration business featuring a sophisticated color palette and stunning visual presentation of ~200 photos.

## Color Palette & Design
- **White** (`#FFFFFF`) — backgrounds and clean space
- **Elegant Pink** — accent color for buttons, highlights, and hover effects
- **Warm Cream** (`#EADDCA`) — secondary backgrounds, section dividers, and warmth
- **Typography**: Elegant serif/sans-serif pairing for a luxurious feel
- **Overall vibe**: Clean, feminine, high-end editorial aesthetic

---

## Navigation Bar
- Logo on the left
- 5 nav links: **Home, Services, Gallery, Rentals, About & Contact**
- **"Book a Consultation"** button on the right (pink accent)
- Sticky header, mobile hamburger menu

---

## Page 1: Home
1. **Hero Section** — Fullscreen background image with overlay text: *"Elegant Event Decor for Every Occasion"* and two buttons: "View Gallery" + "View Services"
2. **Services Grid** — 6 elegant cards (Weddings, Catering, Christmas, Baby Showers/Gender Reveal, Graduations, Birthdays) with images and short descriptions
3. **Our Work** — Showcase section with 4 featured photos in an elegant layout
4. **CTA Section** — *"Let's Start Planning Your Perfect Event"* with "Book a Consultation" button
5. **Footer** — © 2026 Haus of Posh. All rights reserved. Designed & developed by Adam Remaih

---

## Page 2: Services
- A **packages section** (content to be provided later)
- 6 individual service sections, each with:
  - Service image/visual
  - Service name and description
  - Two buttons: **"View Gallery"** (links to Gallery page filtered by that service) + **"Book a Consultation"**

---

## Page 3: Gallery
- Full gallery of ~200 photos
- **Filter tabs** by service type: All, Weddings, Catering, Christmas, Baby Showers/Gender Reveal, Graduations, Birthdays
- Masonry or grid layout with lightbox zoom on click
- Smooth animations on filter change

---

## Page 4: Rentals
- Category-based product catalog with photos, titles, and prices
- **Main categories with sub-categories:**
  - **Weddings & Engagements** → Centre Pieces, Table Cloths, Cake Tables, Chair Covers, Backdrops, Table Runners, Charger Plates, Napkins
  - **Desert Tables**
  - **Christmas**
  - **Birthdays** → Balloons, Backdrops
  - **Graduation**
  - **Gender Reveal / Baby Showers**
  - **Ramadan Decorations**
- Sidebar or tab navigation for categories, with expandable sub-categories
- Each rental item shown as a card with photo, title, and price

---

## Page 5: About & Contact
**About section:**
- A paragraph about Haus of Posh
- Photo of the co-founder

**Contact section (Formspree integration):**
- Inquiry type dropdown: **Booking** or **Rentals**
- Fields: First Name, Last Name, Event Date (date picker), Event Location, Phone Number, Email, Description
- Form submits via **Formspree.io**
- Validation on all fields with clear error messages

---

## Technical Notes
- All images will be provided by the user and uploaded to the project
- No backend/database needed — contact form handled via Formspree.io
- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Smooth scroll animations and hover effects for a premium feel
